SanzAgent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

SanzAgent is a private, internal-use application. It is used solely to publish and manage content on Facebook Pages administered by the application's operator, and to read aggregate engagement statistics for those same Pages.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only Pages managed by its operator: page posts, comments on those posts, and aggregate page insights, using Facebook's Graph API with page-scoped permissions. It does not target, collect, or process personal data of any other Facebook user.

What data the app stores

The app stores only Facebook-issued access tokens and the content it publishes (post text and images). No third-party personal data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with anyone.

Data deletion

The app keeps no data about third parties, so there is normally nothing to delete. Any Facebook user who believes this app holds data about them can request review and deletion by emailing the address below; requests are honored within 30 days.
